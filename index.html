From dentistry to Finance

I started a Bachelor of Dental Surgery at Jamia Millia Islamia in September 2025. In my first year I saw that my interests were closer to business, economics and finance than to clinical practice.

It began in high school. I read macro data releases and reports, and I wanted to know how numbers like Non-Farm Payrolls, ISM and JOLTS move markets and currencies. Over the last couple of years I started trading and kept learning about forex, monetary policy and central bank decisions.

I respected dentistry, but my curiosity was stronger elsewhere. I left in November 2025. Now I study the BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore and a BBA at Jamia Millia Islamia, and I am preparing for CFA Level 1.

I don’t see this as leaving a field. I see it as finding where my interest is and acting on it.

Work

Nocturne Financial Terminal

A single workspace for following the markets. I kept one tab for geopolitical news, another for US Treasury yields, another for forex prices and more. So I built one place for all of it, somewhat inspired by the Bloomberg Terminal.

What I decided: keep everything in one view, and include only what I look at myself when I analyse markets: macro developments, geopolitical events, bond yields and currency prices.

The hardest part was making it feel like one terminal, not a pile of widgets and news feeds.

I made it for myself first, and for anyone who follows markets and wants to connect economic events to price moves.

What it shows: an economic calendar with actual, forecast and previous figures; a bond yield lead-lag panel that compares the US 2-year yield with gold, USD/JPY, EUR/USD and NAS100; a Fed statement comparison; and a news hub filtered by region and central bank.
